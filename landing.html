FAQ

Common questions

Is the free trial really free? No card needed?
Yes — the Trial tier is free forever with 30 questions per day. You don't need a credit card. Upgrade only when you're ready for unlimited practice and full mock exams.
Which certifications and use cases does PracticeBuddy Lab cover?
The platform covers ICD-10-CM, CPT, HCPCS, and E&M coding — relevant for CPC, CCS, COC, and other certification exams. It's also used for on-the-job upskilling and technical interview preparation. Trainers can load custom questions for any specialty or use case.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes. PracticeBuddy Lab is fully mobile-optimised — swipe left to advance cards, swipe right to skip, large touch targets on MCQ options, and horizontal category scroll. Works great on Android and iOS browsers.
How does the institute plan work?
You get a Trainer account that lets you create cohorts, add member access codes, import your question bank, schedule timed exams for your batch, and view per-member mastery analytics. Contact us via WhatsApp for a live demo.
Do you provide the question bank or do we bring our own?
Both. A starter question set is available for trial users. Trainers can import their own questions via CSV with full ICD-10/CPT/HCPCS/E&M tagging — ideal for institutes that already have question banks.
Is pricing available in my local currency?
Yes — contact us via WhatsApp or email and we'll confirm pricing in your preferred currency. Individual plans start at $99/year and institute plans start at $599/year.
